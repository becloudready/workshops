Developer / Project Brief
Project Title: NoticeBoardTracker
----------------------------------------------------------
Client Context: EdTech Organization

Workflow:

Onboarding: HR/People Team onboards new trainees.

Planning: Training Managers assign plans to trainees (organized primarily into group cohorts, alongside occasional solo tracks).

Execution & Feedback: System notifies trainees of updates; trainees submit progress reports directly through the platform.

=======
Pain Points to Resolve:

Scattered 1:1 communication channels

Error-prone, manual Excel tracking

Untracked/missing trainees and duplicated entries

Delayed progress updates and lack of high-level managerial oversight
======
Core Objective: Build a full-stack platform that replaces ad-hoc communications with automated notifications, streamlined progress reporting, and a high-level tracking dashboard for Training Managers.
