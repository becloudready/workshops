# Support

This repository accompanies workshops BeCloudReady delivers. It is not a community support channel.

## If you are in an active cohort

Ask your instructor. That is faster than an issue, and your cohort has a channel for exactly this.

## If you are working through a lab on your own

Issues are triaged, not staffed. We read them and fix real defects in the material, such as a broken command, a stale console path, or a dataset that has moved. Expect a reply in days rather than hours, and expect no reply at all to questions about your own environment.

We do not debug your cloud account, your credentials, or your network. Those are the parts we control in a delivered workshop and cannot see from here.

## If you are evaluating us

The labs are public so you can read them before an engagement. If you want to talk about running one of these with your team, [becloudready.com](https://becloudready.com/workshops?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=workshops&utm_content=support) is the place to start.
